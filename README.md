Finance &amp; Research Portfolio 
### 1. VBL - Financial Model & Valuation
Built a complete financial model and valuation of Varun Beverages Limited (NSE: VBL | BSE: 540180) as an educational project. The model includes a five-year historical analysis (CY21–CY25), ratio analysis, Dupont analysis, the Altman Z-Score, and an intrinsic growth assessment based on ROIC and reinvestment rates. Revenue and costs are forecast for CY26–CY31 across Bear, Base and Bull scenarios, and valued using FCFF-based DCF and comparable company analysis. Results summarised in a Football Field chart.

### 2. Titan Valuation Report
1. 3-statement historical analysis (FY23–FY26) from Annual Reports
2. WACC build-up — Cost of Equity (CAPM) and Cost of Debt from actuals
3. Beta via regression of Titan's daily returns vs Nifty 50 (5Y window)
4. 5-year FCFF forecast with explicit Capex and Working Capital
5. Relative Valuation against listed jewellery peers
6. Football Field chart vs current market price

Assumptions: Rf - India's 10Y G-Sec | ERP - Damodaran India CRP | Beta - own regression | Terminal growth < WACC, near long-run GDP

### 3. Indian FMCG Industry Report
Market size, growth drivers, regulation, competitive landscape, SWOT and financial benchmarking of HUL, ITC, Nestle, Britannia and Dabur.

Key Takeaway: India's FMCG market grew ~14% from US$253.62B in FY24 to US$289.12B in FY25, expected to reach US$642.87B by FY30 (17.33% CAGR). Urban outpacing rural in FY26 vs FY25, rural muted on affordability pressures.
